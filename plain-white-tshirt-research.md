## Two possible positions

**Position A — Everyday essential:** Affordable, plain white shirts with simple sizing, reliable quality, and clear shipping. This is the chosen initial direction.

**Position B — Creative expression:** Artist-made graphic shirts and limited designs. This is an alternative inspired by Threadless, but is less aligned with a plain white T-shirt brief.

## Two persona hypotheses — assumptions to test

**Persona 1: Budget-conscious college student (hypothesis).** Wants a simple shirt for class and everyday wear; values price, comfort, easy sizing, and fast checkout. Possible concern: shipping makes a low price expensive.

**Persona 2: Busy retail worker (hypothesis).** Wants a comfortable basic shirt for work and casual use; values durability, fabric opacity, easy washing, and dependable delivery. Possible concern: fit and quality cannot be judged online.

**Chosen initial audience:** Students and young workers seeking a reliable everyday basic. This is a **hypothesis**, not validated customer research. Test with classmates before finalizing.

## Brand Studio application

- **Archetype:** Everyperson: approachable, practical, inclusive.
- **Design direction:** Modernist: clean white space, readable text, straightforward navigation and size charts.
- **Ethical persuasion:** Real customer reviews as social proof (only if genuine); truthful product details; no invented urgency, reviews, or scarcity.
- **Value proposition hypothesis:** A simple, comfortable white T-shirt with clear sizing and honest total pricing.
- **Main open questions:** What price range is acceptable? What fabric weight do customers prefer? What would make them trust a new store?
